Ring size guide

  1. Cut along the line between point A and the arrow, just long enough for point B to pass through. Don't cut A all the way apart.
  2. Wrap point B around the base of your ring finger, numbers facing out.
  3. Thread B through the slit at A and pull until it sits snug, but slides over your knuckle.
  4. Read the number where the strip meets the arrow. That's your size.
Between two sizes? Choose the half size between them. If it falls between 5 and 6, your size is 5.5. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are a little larger.
